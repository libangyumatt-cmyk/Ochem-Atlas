### Feature registry and edge rule

The reviewed groups are polar proton transfer (including alkene protonation with additional π reorganization), backside substitution, C=O addition, alkene-forming elimination, bromonium formation, tetrahedral carbonyl collapse, heterolytic C–X ionization, and radical atom transfer. Membership is by explicit `reaction-id:zero-based-step` keys in `dist/learning.js`. For example, alkene protonation (`1111`) and direct proton transfer (`1122`) share only the narrower *proton-transfer involvement* feature, not a code or complete arrow topology. E2/E1/E1cB alkene-forming events share the provisional `1133` code and the C=C-forming feature while differing in concertedness and intermediates. An unlisted pair is not linked merely to make a connected graph.

### Source evidence retained and reread

- Kubelka and Bickelhaupt, *J. Phys. Chem. A* **121** (2017), 885–891, [DOI 10.1021/acs.jpca.6b12240](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.6b12240), Tables 3–4: Cl⁻ + A–Cl (A=C,N,O,F), ZORA-OLYP/TZ2P, gas phase; the F entry is a transition complex. The already transcribed σ*(A–Cl) energies (eV) and interaction energies (kcal/mol) are distinct quantities. No value describes HO⁻ + CH₃Br.
- Vermeeren et al., *Chem. Eur. J.* **28** (2022), e202200987, [DOI 10.1002/chem.202200987](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202200987), Table 1 and orbital analysis: butadiene with acrolein or acrolein radical cation at matched C···C 2.203 Å, ZORA-(U)BP86/TZ2P; neutral singlet versus radical-cation doublet. The published radical-cation pathway is stepwise and is not assigned the neutral `1333` code.
- Fernández et al., *J. Chem. Theory Comput.* **19** (2023), 7300–7306, [DOI 10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00907](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00907), Figures 2/9: gas-phase CN⁻ + acetone, M06-2X/6-311+G(d,p) relaxed geometry scan and ZORA-M06-2X/TZ2P EDA. The approximate 111° angle belongs to that model, not a universal Bürgi–Dunitz value or a prediction of facial selectivity.

These sources establish their own model results, not the SORG taxonomy. They were used for interpretive narratives; the v0.2.9 tables and numerical transcriptions were not edited. No project quantum calculations were performed.

## Remaining limitations / research

Fourteen original reaction illustrations remain withheld. The event map only has manually reviewed feature families; many events have code-only links and some features span distinct mechanisms deliberately. The map does not encode kinetics, causal pathways between events, or a computed distance. Fischer's grouped proton relay and the hydride/Grignard work-up stages are not uniquely resolved microscopic events. Candidate `1113` and `1133` remain broad; quantitative validation of assignment boundaries and reaction-specific orbital/energy studies are still needed. Facial stereoselectivity, dynamic trajectories, explicit solvent and conformer populations are not inferred from the three published models. Mobile touch/viewport and deployed caching are untested until a supported real-browser check is performed and recorded.
