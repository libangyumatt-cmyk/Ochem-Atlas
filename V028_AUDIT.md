## Learning and evidence

The “Same code, different chemistry” control compares two **events from different reactions** sharing a code. It exposes their equations, electron flow, orbitals, geometry and assignment limits. The shared-code sentence is deliberately coarse. Codes 1133 and 1113 show their concrete boundaries:

- **1133:** E2 coupling, bromonium formation, E1cB loss and arene attack are all polar multiple-bond reorganizations under the current convention. Their arrow patterns, attacking species and intermediate geometries differ; the existing codebook's base/C–H/C–X example does not literally encompass each case. Keep the code and candidate status until a codebook revision defines its boundary.
- **1113:** Carbonyl attack/collapse changes C=O and planar/tetrahedral geometry; iminium dehydration instead makes C=N. The C=N extension is a provisional analogy and requires an explicit codebook decision later.

All 31 quantum-descriptor pairs are now reaction-specific; repeated generic proton-transfer and carbonyl-addition language was replaced for 27 affected events. The SN2 card distinguishes its HO⁻/CH₃Br teaching equation from the published gas-phase Cl⁻/A–Cl C/N/O/F series. Kubelka and Bickelhaupt, [*J. Phys. Chem. A* 121 (2017), 885–891](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.6b12240), Tables 3–4: 12 transcribed numerical cells; ZORA-OLYP/TZ2P, −1 singlet fragments, with F a transition complex.

The Diels–Alder card distinguishes neutral butadiene/ethene from butadiene with neutral or radical-cation acrolein. Vermeeren et al., [*Chem. Eur. J.* 28 (2022), e202200987](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202200987), Table 1: eight transcribed strain/interaction/Pauli/orbital cells at the matched 2.203 Å geometry, ZORA-(U)BP86/TZ2P, gas phase. The radical-cation path is a separate doublet, stepwise mechanism; its numbers and 1333 code are not transferred to the neutral ethene card.

The generic carbonyl card now foregrounds the published ~111° CN⁻/acetone trajectory as **analogous**. Fernández et al., [*J. Chem. Theory Comput.* 19 (2023), 7300–7306](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c00907), Computational Methods and Figures 2/9: M06-2X/6-311+G(d,p) geometry scan; ZORA-M06-2X/TZ2P energy decomposition, gas-phase −1 singlet model. The reported small-angle Pauli contribution and larger-angle deformation cost do not establish a universal approach angle for an unspecified R₂C=O + Nu⁻ pair.

The original literature tables and numerical fields are unchanged. Their full source-check scope and the unexecuted protocols are recorded in [the prior audit](AUDIT.md). No orbital energies, barriers or coupling constants were newly invented.

## Validation and remaining limits

`npm test` checks 31 IDs, 69 event records, unchanged codebook, linked local figures, SVG labels and pair-arrow inventories, and DOM comparison behavior across all selectable codes. The existing filters, search, saves, panels and routes remain in the suite. The five SVGs were rasterized and visually checked; this confirms legibility and charge/equation consistency, not experimental pathway uniqueness. GitHub Pages and served-asset verification are recorded in the session handoff after publication.

Native desktop/mobile browser rendering, touch gestures, image loading and real-browser console checks remain **untested** because the managed environment does not expose the supported browser-control workflow. The long mechanisms have responsive scrolling styles, but no mobile visual pass is claimed. Fourteen illustration references remain withheld. Further research should resolve solvent-mediated Fischer relays, alternative imine formation paths and the 1133/1113 codebook boundaries without altering this release's numeric taxonomy.
